FAQ

Questions

When is Aurel coming out?

Soon. We’re polishing it now. Join the waitlist and you’ll be one of the first to get it — we’ll send one email when it’s ready.

How much will it cost?

Pricing will be shared before launch — waitlist members hear first.

Does it work on Windows or iPhone?

Aurel is built for Mac first. Other platforms may follow based on demand.

Do I need Edsby or Google Classroom?

No. They’re optional connections. Aurel works great as an everyday browser, and gets smarter as you connect your accounts.

Can I use Aurel for my business?

Yes. The Business Hub handles clients, jobs, invoices and expenses for small businesses and side hustles. Everything is stored on your Mac, and invoices are saved as PDFs you send yourself.

Will my websites work normally?

Yes. Aurel runs on the same engine as Chrome, so the sites you use every day work just like you’re used to.
